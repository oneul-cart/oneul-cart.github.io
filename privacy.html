Privacy Policy — Shorts Factory (shorts-uploader)

What we access

When an account owner connects a TikTok account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through TikTok Login Kit, and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How we use it

Only to upload our own videos to that same account as drafts, and to show which account is connected. We do not read, collect or analyze other users' data, and we do not sell or share any data.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer in a local settings file. They are never published, logged in plain text, or sent to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. Revoking access in TikTok (Security → Manage app permissions) stops all access; we then delete the stored token. Requests: converse1004@gmail.com.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the operator.
