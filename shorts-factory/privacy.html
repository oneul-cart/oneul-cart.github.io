Privacy Policy

SHORTS FACTORY · Effective 2 October 2026

1. Who operates this application

SHORTS FACTORY is operated by a single individual for personal use. Contact: converse1004@gmail.com

2. What the application does

SHORTS FACTORY uploads short videos that the operator has produced on their own computer to the operator’s own YouTube channels, along with the title, description, tags and thumbnail prepared for each video. The operator is the only user. The application is not offered to the public.

3. YouTube API Services

SHORTS FACTORY uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator’s own YouTube channel. By using it, the operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google’s own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The operator can revoke this application’s access to their Google account at any time through the Google security settings page, https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Google user data we access

The application requests one scope and no others:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload a video to the authorising account’s YouTube channel and to set that video’s thumbnail.

This scope is used for one purpose only: publishing the operator’s own videos to the operator’s own channel. The application does not read channel analytics, subscriber lists, comments, watch history, playlists, email, contacts, or any other Google account data.

5. What we store

The application collects no personal information and operates no server, database or analytics.

The OAuth credentials and the refresh token issued by Google are stored as files on the operator’s own computer, are readable only by the operator’s own operating-system account, and are never transmitted anywhere other than to Google when authenticating a request. Video files and their metadata stay on the operator’s computer until they are uploaded to YouTube.

6. Sharing

No data of any kind is sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to any third party. There are no advertisers, no analytics providers and no subprocessors.

7. Limited Use

SHORTS FACTORY’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and deletion

The operator can revoke the application’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token immediately. Deleting the local credential and token files removes every trace of the authorisation from the computer. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, no further deletion request is necessary; one may still be sent to the contact address above.

9. Children

The application is not directed to children and is used only by its operator.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
