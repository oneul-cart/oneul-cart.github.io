SHORTS FACTORY

A personal publishing tool for the owner’s own YouTube channels

What this is

SHORTS FACTORY is a private, single-user tool. It takes short videos that the owner has produced on their own computer and uploads them to the owner’s own YouTube channels, together with the title, description, tags and thumbnail prepared for each video.

It is not a public service. No one other than the owner signs in to it, and it does not accept content from anyone else.

What it accesses

It requests exactly one Google permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload a video and set its thumbnail

It does not read channel analytics, subscriber information, comments, or any other account data.

Privacy

The tool collects, stores and shares no personal information. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
